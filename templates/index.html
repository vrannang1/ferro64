Use a vendor's models with the coding agent. These are not local: while one is selected, your project's code goes to that vendor. They also get expensive, because every step resends the whole conversation and you pay per token, so a local model or a flat subscription is the better everyday choice. Keep these for when you are truly stuck. Keys are kept on this machine, in Ferro64's database, and are never shown again.
